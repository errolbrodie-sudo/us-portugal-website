Embassy of Portugal in Washington, D.C.

The official diplomatic mission of the Portuguese Republic to the United States of America. The U.S. Department of State recognizes the Embassy in Washington, D.C., alongside 6 regional Consular offices in Boston, New Bedford, New York, Newark, Providence, and San Francisco.

⚠️ IMPORTANT — Before Booking a Portugal Visa Appointment
Portugal visa applications in the United States are subject to strict jurisdiction rules. Applicants must submit their application at the appropriate VFS Visa Application Centre designated for their permanent place of residence. VFS Global currently requires applicants to appear in person for biometric data capture and physical dossier submission.

Portugal Visa Application Centres in the USA (VFS Global)

VFS Global is the official partner of the Embassy of Portugal in the USA for visa processing. VFS operates physical application submission centres across key regional hubs in the United States.

Jurisdiction Notice: VFS locations and jurisdiction rules can change. Verify the current location and jurisdiction before booking an appointment.
